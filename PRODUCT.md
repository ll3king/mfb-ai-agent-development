# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Australian founder-led agency owners and small agency leadership teams.

They are usually the operational routing layer inside their business: inbox escalation, approvals, follow-up, handoffs, status chasing, and decision bottlenecks still flow through them.

They are not shopping for an AI toy. They want a practical way to remove founder-led operational drag without hiring blindly or adding another generic software tool.

## Product Purpose

This landing page sells a done-for-you `AI Chief of Staff implementation` service.

The service designs and installs a founder-specific AI operating layer across intake, routing, follow-up, approvals, and visibility. The page's job is to stop attention fast, make the bottleneck legible in seconds, and convert qualified founders into a strategy call or implementation conversation.

Success looks like:

- the buyer immediately recognizes their founder bottleneck
- the offer feels concrete, premium, and implementation-led
- the page avoids generic AI-agent positioning
- the CTA feels worth taking now

## Brand Personality

- sharp
- controlled
- high-stakes

The page should feel like an operational intervention, not a friendly AI explainer.

Emotional goals:

- pressure recognized quickly
- authority without hype
- relief through structure

## Anti-references

- generic AI agency pages
- dashboard-explainer hero sections with too much copy
- editorial-magazine landing pages with default serif drama
- safe SaaS pages that lead with features instead of the buyer's bottleneck
- heavy video-first pages that hurt mobile load speed

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the founder bottleneck, not the AI concept.
2. One decisive visual idea per fold beats dense explanation.
3. Show operational control, not generic automation.
4. Keep the page light enough to load fast on mobile.
5. Every section must either sharpen the pain, clarify the mechanism, or move the buyer toward the CTA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility.
- Respect reduced motion preferences.
- Keep copy scannable and concise on mobile.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate state or meaning.

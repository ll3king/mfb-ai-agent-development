# Design

## Overview

This is a dark, image-led service landing page for a premium B2B implementation offer.

The page should feel sharper, cleaner, and more controlled than the previous version. It should look like a decisive operational intervention, not an AI agency template.

## Visual Direction

- campaign-grade hero, not SaaS dashboard explainer
- one dominant image with minimal overlays
- condensed sans display typography
- one primary accent across the page
- section rhythm built from contrast and spacing, not repeated card systems

## Typography

- display direction: condensed sans
- body direction: neutral sans
- no editorial serif
- no italicized serif emphasis
- hero headline should stay under two lines on desktop

## CTA

- one primary CTA label across the whole page: `Book Your Bottleneck Audit`
- secondary actions may navigate within the page, but should not compete with the primary action

## Imagery

- one decisive hero image
- overlays must stay sparse
- the image should communicate founder pressure and operational control in the same frame

## Layout

- top half must carry the selling load
- section headers should stack vertically, not use split-header scaffolding
- remove fake trust devices such as placeholder logo walls
- replace repeated cards with rows, bands, or structured lists where possible

## Performance

- keep the page lightweight
- avoid additional heavy assets unless they materially improve the hero
- if one image is used, it should carry most of the emotional weight
